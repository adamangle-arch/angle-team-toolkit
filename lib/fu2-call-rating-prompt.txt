Process Context

The FU2 call sits inside a structured multi-step process:

1. Pre-filter

Short call used to:
	•	establish basic interest
	•	check if the person is open to creating income outside what they currently do
	•	begin building trust and positioning

2. QI1

First substantial vetting conversation — diagnostic and trust-building. Compensation is deliberately avoided here.

3. QI2
	•	Book review
	•	Macro run-through of the business model
	•	Context is built before introducing the network marketing structure

4. Webinar

Deep dive into:
	•	business model
	•	mentorship system

5. Audios before FU1

Prospect listens to 4–5 audios.

6. FU1

Focus on:
	•	commitments
	•	habits
	•	expectations
	•	PV commitment

7. More education
	•	additional audios
	•	another webinar

8. FU2 (this call)

Compensation plan run-through.

Goals of FU2:
	•	walk through the actual compensation plan in real detail
	•	tie the numbers back to the candidate's own stated financial goals from QI1 — the gap between where they are and where they want to be
	•	answer detailed questions about how income is actually earned
	•	check genuine understanding of the key mechanics, not just deliver the plan

FU2 is not meant to:
	•	be the first time compensation has ever come up — by design QI1 and QI2 avoided it, so this should land as the natural payoff of that build-up, not an out-of-nowhere info dump
	•	replace the financial operating blueprint (that's FU3, later)

9. Ideally attend an in-person event

10. More learning
	•	more audios
	•	another book
	•	another webinar

11. FU3

Financial operating blueprint:
	•	costs
	•	operating structure
	•	launch fees

12. 18-part questionnaire

13. Final call

Short call to:
	•	answer final questions
	•	formally extend a partnership offer

Funnel Context

This is a long vetting process designed to prioritize quality over quantity.

Key points:
	•	Pre-filtering should already create trust and positioning
	•	Most people who reach QI1 will normally continue forward
	•	The more important metric is who launches and builds

Typical results:
	•	About 3–3.5 out of 10 prospects launch
	•	Of those who launch:
	•	one becomes a strong builder
	•	one incubates and builds later
	•	one mainly engages in personal development
	•	one likely does not continue

Retention is high because expectations are set progressively throughout the process.
