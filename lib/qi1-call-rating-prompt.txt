Process Context

The QI1 call sits inside a structured multi-step process:

1. Pre-filter

Short call used to:
	•	establish basic interest
	•	check if the person is open to creating income outside what they currently do
	•	begin building trust and positioning

2. QI1 (this call)

First substantial vetting conversation.

Goals of QI1:
	•	deepen trust
	•	assess trust velocity
	•	understand the person's ambitions and long-term thinking
	•	test outcome focus vs passion orientation
	•	test patience and long-term mindset
	•	assess coachability and teachability
	•	assess openness to mentorship
	•	explore previous exposure to network marketing / direct sales
	•	see whether they can approach this with a fresh slate
	•	understand the gap between where they are financially and where they want to be

QI1 is not meant to:
	•	explain the whole business
	•	close the person
	•	go deep into compensation

QI1 is mainly a diagnostic and trust-building call.

Funnel Context

This is a long vetting process designed to prioritize quality over quantity.

Key points:
	•	Pre-filtering should already create trust and positioning
	•	Most people who reach QI1 will normally continue forward
	•	The more important metric is who launches and builds

Typical results:
	•	About 3–3.5 out of 10 prospects launch
	•	Of those who launch:
	•	one becomes a strong builder
	•	one incubates and builds later
	•	one mainly engages in personal development
	•	one likely does not continue

Retention is high because expectations are set progressively throughout the process.

1. Overall QI1 score

Score the call out of 10 as an QI1 inside this process.

Score Calibration — anchor the number to this scale rather than intuition

	•	9-10: Outstanding — real trust built fast, sharp diagnostic questions

	•	7-8: Strong — genuine rapport and real diagnostic value, natural

	•	5-6: Solid but with real gaps — connection was there, but the call

	opportunities pass unaddressed.
	•	3-4: Below the bar — meaningful problems with connection, pacing, or
	diagnosis that would concern an experienced upline.
	•	1-2: Poor — little real vetting happened.

A call with good rapport, real openness from the candidate, and natural
forward momentum belongs in the 7-8 range even with a few specific things
to sharpen — reserve 5-6 and below for calls with actual structural gaps
(multiple diagnostic goals for this stage untouched, weak connection, no
real pressure-testing at all), not just room for polish. Don't let a
genuinely good call get dragged into "average" territory over a handful
of minor, specific critiques.

3. What to improve

2-3 specific, actionable things to sharpen next time — each one tied to

Calibration note: the IBO does need to talk and explain a meaningful
amount — the prospect usually has no idea what's going on yet, and some
explanation is normal and necessary, especially the further along the
process goes (QI2 and FU1 in particular require substantially more
explanation than QI1). Do not treat talk time or explaining itself as a
weakness. Only flag it when it comes at the expense of connection or
diagnostic quality — e.g. the IBO explaining instead of asking, or missing
a chance to find out something real about the candidate. The bar is: are
they still connecting with the candidate, and are they still asking good
questions along the way — not how much they talked.

Look for things like:
	•	teaching instead of extracting when a question would have worked better
	•	missed diagnostic opportunities
	•	weak follow-up questions
	•	low challenge or pressure testing
	•	philosophy or storytelling that goes on past the point of serving connection or diagnosis
	•	poor pacing
	•	unclear transitions
	•	energy drops
	•	losing connection with the candidate while explaining
