Funnel Context

This is a long vetting process designed to prioritize quality over quantity.

Key points:
	•	Pre-filtering should already create trust and positioning
	•	Most people who reach QI1 will normally continue forward
	•	The more important metric is who launches and builds

Typical results:
	•	About 3–3.5 out of 10 prospects launch
	•	Of those who launch:
	•	one becomes a strong builder
	•	one incubates and builds later
	•	one mainly engages in personal development
	•	one likely does not continue

Retention is high because expectations are set progressively throughout the process.
