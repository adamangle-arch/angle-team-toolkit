Process Context

The Questionnaire call sits inside a structured multi-step process:

1. Pre-filter

Short call used to:
	•	establish basic interest
	•	check if the person is open to creating income outside what they currently do
	•	begin building trust and positioning

2. QI1

First substantial vetting conversation — diagnostic and trust-building.

3. QI2
	•	Book review
	•	Macro run-through of the business model
	•	Context is built before introducing the network marketing structure

4. Webinar

Deep dive into:
	•	business model
	•	mentorship system

5. Audios before FU1

Prospect listens to 4–5 audios.

6. FU1

Focus on:
	•	commitments
	•	habits
	•	expectations
	•	PV commitment

7. More education
	•	additional audios
	•	another webinar

8. FU2

Compensation plan run-through.

9. Ideally attend an in-person event

10. More learning
	•	more audios
	•	another book
	•	another webinar

11. FU3

Financial operating blueprint:
	•	costs
	•	operating structure
	•	launch fees

12. 18-part questionnaire (this call)

Goals of the Questionnaire call:
	•	go through all 18 parts of the questionnaire together
	•	draw out genuine, specific answers rather than quick or rehearsed ones
	•	surface any final hesitations, doubts, or misalignments before the Final call
	•	cross-check answers against everything learned about this candidate since QI1 — do they hold up, or contradict what came before
	•	assess real readiness for a partnership offer

The Questionnaire call is not meant to:
	•	be the close itself — that's the Final call, immediately after
	•	introduce new business, compensation, or financial content — all of that should already be covered by this point
	•	accept surface-level answers at face value without probing

By this stage in the process, the candidate should already understand the business, the compensation, and the financial operating blueprint — this call is about listening and extracting, not teaching.

13. Final call

Short call to:
	•	answer final questions
	•	formally extend a partnership offer

Funnel Context

This is a long vetting process designed to prioritize quality over quantity.

Key points:
	•	Pre-filtering should already create trust and positioning
	•	Most people who reach QI1 will normally continue forward
	•	The more important metric is who launches and builds

Typical results:
	•	About 3–3.5 out of 10 prospects launch
	•	Of those who launch:
	•	one becomes a strong builder
	•	one incubates and builds later
	•	one mainly engages in personal development
	•	one likely does not continue

Retention is high because expectations are set progressively throughout the process.
